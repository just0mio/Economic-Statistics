 C2. Pest warning test

 (a) 전확률법칙: P(+)=0.20×0.80+0.80×0.10=0.16+0.08=0.24.


 (b) 베이즈 정리: P(D|+)=0.20×0.80/0.24=0.16/0.24=2/3≈0.6667.


 (c) P(-|D)=0.20, P(-|D^c)=0.90. P(D|-)=(0.20×0.20)/(0.20×0.20+0.80×0.90)=0.04/0.76≈0.0526.



 C3. Catering orders and waiting time

 (a) E(X)=0×.1+1×.4+2×.4+3×.1=1.5. E(X²)=0+.4+4×.4+9×.1=2.9. Var(X)=2.9-1.5²=0.65, sd(X)=√0.65≈0.8062.


 (b) E(Y)=20E(X)-5=20×1.5-5=25(천 원). sd(Y)=|20|sd(X)≈20×0.8062=16.12(천 원).


 (c) K~Binomial(3,0.1). P(K=1)=C(3,1)×0.1×0.9²=3×0.1×0.81=0.243.


 (d) P(T>40)=(50-40)/(50-10)=10/40=0.25. 75백분위수=10+0.75(50-10)=40분.



  FINAL FORMULA & ERROR CHECK
   Descriptive                 Sample variance s²=Σ(xi-x-bar)²/(n-1); sd=√s²; CV=sd/x-bar ×100%; IQR=Q3-Q1.

   Probability                 P(A ∪ B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A ∩ B); P(A|B)=P(A ∩ B)/P(B).

   Bayes                       P(D|+)=P(+|D)P(D)/[P(+|D)P(D)+P(+|D^c)P(D^c)].

   Moments                     E(X)=Σx p(x); Var(X)=E(X²)-[E(X)]²; E(a+bX)=a+bE(X); Var(a+bX)=b²Var(X).

   Distributions               Binomial: E=np, Var=np(1-p). Poisson: P(X=k)=exp(-λ) × λ^k/k!, E=Var=λ.

   Continuous                  Uniform(a,b): P(c<X<d)=(d-c)/(b-a). Exponential(rate λ): P(T>t)=exp(-λt).
