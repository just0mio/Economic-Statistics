 C2. Two crop-pest tests

 (a) P(+)=0.12×0.85+0.88×0.10=0.102+0.088=0.190.


 (b) P(D|+)=0.102/0.190≈0.5368. 양성이라는 정보가 감염 확률을 12%에서 약 53.7%로 높인다.


 (c) 감염 여부를 조건으로 두 검사 결과가 독립이므로 P(+,+|D)=0.85², P(+,+|D^c)=0.10². 따라서
 P(D|+,+)=(0.12×0.85²)/(0.12×0.85²+0.88×0.10²)=0.0867/0.0955≈0.9079.



 C3. Delivery orders, profit, and preparation

 (a) E(X)=0.35+2×0.35+3×0.15=1.5. E(X²)=0.35+4×0.35+9×0.15=3.10. Var(X)=3.10-2.25=0.85,
 sd(X)=√0.85≈0.9220.


 (b) E(Y)=30×1.5-12=33(천 원). sd(Y)=30√0.85≈27.659(천 원). 비용 -12는 표준편차에 영향을 주지 않는다.


 (c) K~Binomial(4,0.2). P(K=2)=C(4,2)×0.2²×0.8²=6×0.04×0.64=0.1536.


 (d) Z=(62-50)/8=1.5 → P(T>62)=1-Φ(1.5)=1-0.9332=0.0668. 90백분위수=50+1.2816×8≈60.2528분.



  FINAL FORMULA & ERROR CHECK
   Descriptive                 Sample variance s²=Σ(xi-x-bar)²/(n-1); sd=√s²; CV=sd/x-bar ×100%; IQR=Q3-Q1.

   Probability                 P(A ∪ B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A ∩ B); P(A|B)=P(A ∩ B)/P(B).

   Bayes                       P(D|+)=P(+|D)P(D)/[P(+|D)P(D)+P(+|D^c)P(D^c)].

   Moments                     E(X)=Σx p(x); Var(X)=E(X²)-[E(X)]²; E(a+bX)=a+bE(X); Var(a+bX)=b²Var(X).

   Distributions               Binomial: E=np, Var=np(1-p). Poisson: P(X=k)=exp(-λ) × λ^k/k!, E=Var=λ.

   Continuous                  Uniform(a,b): P(c<X<d)=(d-c)/(b-a). Exponential(rate λ): P(T>t)=exp(-λt).
