  C1 · A farm independently checks 12 irrigation controllers. Each has probability 0.10 of failure. Let X be the
  number of failed controllers.

  (a) Find P(X=2).

     X~Binomial(12,.1). P(X=2)=C(12,2)×.1²×.9¹ =66×.01×.34867844≈0.23013.


  (b) Find P(X≥1).

     P(X≥1)=1 .9¹²≈1 .28242954=.71757.


  (c) Find E(X), Var(X), and sd(X).

     E(X)=12(.1)=1.2, Var(X)=12(.1)(.9)=1.08, sd(X)=√1.08≈1.0392.


  C2 · Crop disease prevalence is P(D)=0.04. Each diagnostic test has sensitivity P(+|D)=0.92 and false-positive
  rate P(+|D )=0.07. Two tests are conditionally independent given disease status.

  (a) Find P(+) for the first test.

     전확률: P(+)=.04(.92)+.96(.07)=.0368+.0672=.1040.


  (b) Find P(D|+) after one positive test.

     베이즈: P(D|+)=.0368/.104≈.35385.


  (c) Find P(D|+,+) after two positive tests.

     조건부 독립성으로 분자는 .04×.92²=.033856, 비감염 경로는 .96×.07²=.004704.
     후행확률=.033856/(.033856+.004704)=.033856/.03856≈.87801.


  C3 · A food-producer’s net operating return X (thousand KRW) has PMF: x= 30,10,50,90 with probabilities
  0.1,0.4,0.3,0.2. Let Y=1.5X+10. Independent input cost C has E(C)=8 and sd(C)=6; total net profit
  Z=1.5X C+10.

  (a) Compute E(X), E(X²), Var(X), and sd(X).

     E(X)= 30(.1)+10(.4)+50(.3)+90(.2)= 3+4+15+18=34. E(X²)=900(.1)+100(.4)+2500(.3)+8100(.2)=2500.
     Var(X)=2500 34²=1344, sd≈36.6606.


  (b) Compute E(Y), Var(Y), and sd(Y).

     E(Y)=1.5×34+10=61. Var(Y)=1.5²×1344=3024, sd(Y)=√3024≈54.9909.


  (c) Compute E(Z), Var(Z), and sd(Z), showing why independence matters.

     E(Z)=1.5×34 8+10=53. Var(Z)=1.5²×1344+Var(C) 3Cov(X,C)=3024+36=3060 (독립 → Cov=0).
     sd(Z)=√3060≈55.3173.


  (d) Find P(X≥50) and interpret it.

     P(X≥50)=P(X=50)+P(X=90)=.3+.2=.5. 운영수익이 50천원 이상일 확률이 50%다.
