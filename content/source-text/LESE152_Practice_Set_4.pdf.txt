 C1 · Daily visitors to a produce market (sorted): 12, 14, 15, 17, 19, 21, 23, 39.

 (a) Compute the sample mean and median. What does their comparison suggest?

 합계=160, n=8 → 평균=20. 중앙값=(17+19)/2=18. 큰 값 39가 평균을 끌어올려 우측 치우침을 시사.

 (b) Find Q1 and Q3 using Lp=(p/100)(n+1) with linear interpolation. Apply the upper 1.5×IQR fence.

 L25=2.25 → Q1=14+0.25(15 14)=14.25. L75=6.75 → Q3=21+0.75(23 21)=22.5. IQR=8.25. 상한
 =22.5+1.5(8.25)=34.875; 39는 잠재적 이상치.

 (c) Calculate sample variance, sample sd, and coefficient of variation.

 평균 20. 제곱편차합=64+36+25+9+1+1+9+361=506. s²=506/7≈72.2857, s≈8.5021, CV≈8.5021/20×100≈42.51%.

 C2 · Three processing lines supply 50%, 30%, and 20% of goods. Their defect rates are 2%, 5%, and 10%,
 respectively.

 (a) Find the unconditional probability that an item is defective.

 P(D)=.50(.02)+.30(.05)+.20(.10)=.01+.015+.02=.045 (4.5%).

 (b) Given that a randomly sampled item is defective, find P(Line 3 | defective).

 Bayes: P(L3|D)=P(D|L3)P(L3)/P(D)=.10×.20/.045=.02/.045=4/9≈.4444.

 (c) Given that an item is not defective, find P(Line 1 | not defective).

 P(L1|D )=.50(.98)/P(D )=.49/.955≈.5131.

 E(X)=0+.2+1+.6=1.8. E(X²)=.2+4(.5)+9(.2)=4.0. Var(X)=4 1.8²=.76. sd≈.8718.
